Resting membrane potential (RMP)

RMP is the voltage difference across a cell membrane at rest. It reflects ion gradients, selective membrane permeability—especially to K⁺ in many neurons—and the Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase's role in maintaining gradients.

Cell membrane transport

Passive transport moves substances down a gradient without direct energy input. Active transport uses energy directly or indirectly. Vesicular transport includes endocytosis and exocytosis.

Enzymes

Enzymes speed up reactions by lowering activation energy. They do not change the overall free-energy change or equilibrium position of a reaction.

Histology slide approach

Start with the overall tissue pattern, identify the lining or dominant cells, look for distinctive structures, then support your identification with key features.
